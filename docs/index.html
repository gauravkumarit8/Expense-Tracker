Why it works differently

Most expense trackers ask for SMS access or a bank login. This one doesn't need either — and that was a deliberate decision, not a missing feature.

Auto-capture

Bank and UPI notifications are parsed the instant they land — not just SMS, which is where most trackers stop and where they miss transactions.

Budgets that warn you early

Set a monthly limit per category and get told you're on track to cross it — before it happens, not after.

Reminders that find themselves

Recurring bills and subscriptions are detected from your own history — including when a subscription quietly raises its price.

Encrypted, on-device, always

Your transaction history is stored with on-device encryption. There's no server to breach, because there's no server at all.

Backfill with a bank statement

Import a CSV statement from your bank to bring in history from before you installed the app.

Full control, always

Export to CSV, back up and restore locally, or erase everything on the device with one tap. It's your data.
