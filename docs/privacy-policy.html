Privacy Policy — Auto Expense Tracker

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Auto Expense Tracker ("the app") is an Android app that helps you track your spending automatically. This page explains what information the app accesses, what it does with it, and what it never does. We wrote this to be accurate and specific to how the app actually works, not generic boilerplate.

The short version

Your financial transaction data is processed and stored only on your device, encrypted. We do not operate a server, and your transaction history is never uploaded anywhere, ever. The only network activity the app performs is talking to Google's own services — for optional ads, optional subscriptions, and update checks — none of which involve your financial data.

What the app reads

With your permission (Android's "Notification Access" setting, which you must explicitly grant and can revoke at any time), the app reads the text of notifications from your banking, UPI, and default messaging apps, specifically to detect transaction alerts (e.g. "Rs.500 debited...", "You received Rs.1200..."). It does not read notifications from any other app — social media, chat apps, or anything unrelated to financial transaction alerts.

From a matching notification, the app extracts only:

  • The transaction amount
  • Whether it was money sent or received
  • The date/time
  • The counterparty name or account, where present in the message
  • The account balance, where the message happens to include one

The original notification text itself is discarded immediately after these details are extracted. It is never saved to disk, never logged, and never leaves your device.

The app does not use SMS permissions of any kind. It never requests READ_SMS or RECEIVE_SMS. Notification Access is the only mechanism used to detect transactions.

What the app stores

The structured details above (amount, direction, date, counterparty, category, your own notes/tags) are stored in an encrypted database on your device, using industry-standard encryption (SQLCipher) with a key held in your device's own secure hardware (Android Keystore) where available. This data is never transmitted off your device — there is no backend server for this app to send it to.

You can export your data at any time (JSON backup, or CSV for spreadsheet use) directly to a location you choose on your own device or cloud storage — this is an action you take deliberately; the app does not do this automatically or without your action.

You can permanently delete all app data at any time from Settings.

What we never do

  • We do not operate a server that receives your transaction data.
  • We do not sell, share, or transmit your financial data to any third party.
  • We do not use your transaction data for advertising targeting.
  • We do not read notifications from apps unrelated to financial transactions.

Data that Google's own services process

The app uses a small number of Google services, which is the only network access the app has. This is separate from, and does not involve, your financial transaction data:

  • Google AdMob (for the free tier's banner ads): processes an advertising identifier for ad delivery, subject to your ad-consent choice (EU/UK users are shown a consent form on first launch, per Google's requirements, and can change this choice any time from Settings → Privacy & Ad Consent).
  • Google Play Billing (for the optional Pro subscription): processes your purchase/subscription status. Payment details are handled entirely by Google Play — this app never sees or stores your payment information.
  • Google Play's in-app update and in-app review features: check for app updates and, occasionally, prompt for a Play Store rating. Standard Google Play services, not custom infrastructure of ours.

See Google's own Privacy Policy for how these Google services handle data: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Permissions the app uses, and why

  • Notification access — to detect bank/UPI transaction alerts, as described above.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — to show you bill reminders and unusual-spending alerts you've configured.
  • Biometric/fingerprint — only if you turn on the optional app lock feature, to unlock the app.
  • Internet & network state — used only for the Google services described above; never for transmitting your transaction data.

Children's privacy

This app is a personal finance utility intended for general adult use and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

  • You can grant or revoke Notification Access at any time from Android Settings.
  • You can change your ad-consent choice at any time from the app's Settings screen.
  • You can export or permanently delete all your data at any time from the app's Settings screen.
